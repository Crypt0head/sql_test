Dataset: sbx_da.tmp_shestopalov_acq_datamart_jan_jun
Перед использованием: Dataset → Sync columns (должна быть колонка active_retl_cnt).

Правило:
- retl_cnt / active_retl_cnt  = торговые точки (ТТ)
- term_cnt / active_terms     = терминалы
- НЕ использовать SUM(active_terms) и НЕ умножать на 0.9 для «активных ТТ»


==============================================================================
ТОРГОВЫЕ ТОЧКИ
==============================================================================

merchants_total — Всего торговых точек
SUM(COALESCE(CAST(NULLIF(BTRIM(CAST(retl_cnt AS TEXT)), '') AS NUMERIC), 0))

merchants_active — Активные торговые точки
SUM(COALESCE(CAST(NULLIF(BTRIM(CAST(active_retl_cnt AS TEXT)), '') AS NUMERIC), 0))

merchants_passive — Неактивные торговые точки
SUM(COALESCE(CAST(NULLIF(BTRIM(CAST(retl_cnt AS TEXT)), '') AS NUMERIC), 0))
-
SUM(COALESCE(CAST(NULLIF(BTRIM(CAST(active_retl_cnt AS TEXT)), '') AS NUMERIC), 0))


==============================================================================
ТЕРМИНАЛЫ
==============================================================================

terminals_total — Всего терминалов
SUM(COALESCE(CAST(NULLIF(BTRIM(CAST(term_cnt AS TEXT)), '') AS NUMERIC), 0))

terminals_active — Активные терминалы
SUM(COALESCE(CAST(NULLIF(BTRIM(CAST(active_terms AS TEXT)), '') AS NUMERIC), 0))

terminals_passive — Неактивные терминалы
SUM(COALESCE(CAST(NULLIF(BTRIM(CAST(term_cnt AS TEXT)), '') AS NUMERIC), 0))
-
SUM(COALESCE(CAST(NULLIF(BTRIM(CAST(active_terms AS TEXT)), '') AS NUMERIC), 0))
